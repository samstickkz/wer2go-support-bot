## About wer2 GO
- wer2 GO is a ride-hailing platform operating in Doha, Qatar.
- Customers book rides through the wer2 GO mobile app (iOS & Android).
- Website: wer2go.com

## Booking a ride
- Download the wer2 GO app, register with your phone number.
- Set pickup and destination, choose ride type, confirm booking.
- A nearby driver accepts and you can track them in the app.
- If no driver accepts the ride, it is cancelled after a wait of up to 20 minutes.

## Ride types & fares (QAR)
- Saver: minimum fare 6.30, 0.69 per km (minimum 4 km), 0.20 per minute. Active.
- Standard: minimum fare 7.65, 0.72 per km (minimum 4 km), 0.22 per minute. Active.
- Comfort and Premium: not yet active (coming soon).
- The app always shows the exact price before booking — the bot may share the rates
  above but must not promise an exact total for a specific trip.

## Payments
- Payment methods: cash or card in-app.
- If charged incorrectly, the support team reviews and refunds where applicable.

## Cancellations
- Rider (customer): free cancellation within 1 minute of booking. After that,
  a cancellation fee of the minimum tariff applies.
- Driver: free cancellation within 30 seconds of accepting a ride; otherwise a
  QAR 5 cancellation penalty applies. After arriving at pickup, the driver may
  cancel free of charge after waiting 2 minutes.
- If a driver cancels on a customer, the customer is not charged.

## Lost items
- Customer should provide trip date/time, pickup & drop-off, and item description.
- Support contacts the driver to arrange return.

## Becoming a driver
- Requirements: valid Qatar driving licence, vehicle meeting wer2 GO standards,
  required transport permits.
- Apply via the wer2 GO Driver app or at the Doha office.
- Verification is required before driving: either physical verification at the
  office or an official recorded video call.

## Driver earnings, commission & wallet
- Standard commission: 10% per ride (drivers keep 90% of the fare).
- Weekly payouts.
- Wallet rules: minimum top-up 1 QAR; minimum payout 150 QAR; withdrawal fee 2.5%.
- Drivers can accept rides as long as wallet balance is above −10 QAR; once the
  balance reaches −10 QAR it must be settled before receiving new rides.

## Driver documents
- When a document (e.g. QID) expires, the driver is notified and has a 1-month
  grace period to submit the renewed document.
- If not submitted after the grace period, the account is temporarily blocked
  until the document is provided.

## Account change requests (drivers)
- Profile/vehicle change requests cost QAR 10, charged from the driver wallet.
- The driver must top up first if the balance is insufficient, and the account
  is temporarily suspended until verification of the change is completed.

## Referral program
- Referral bonus can only be claimed after the referred user completes 5 rides.

## Fair use & fraud (zero tolerance)
- Fake or coordinated rides to exploit promotions/referrals are strictly forbidden.
- Offenses lead to escalating account locks (48 hours, then 7 days) with penalty
  fees, and permanent ban for repeated abuse.
- Disciplinary actions range from a warning (minor) to promo removal, temporary
  suspension, and permanent ban (severe/fraud).

## Support hours
- Support team available daily. Escalated chats are answered as soon as possible.

## Things the bot must NOT do
- Promise an exact total fare for a specific trip, or promise refund amounts
  or compensation (escalate instead).
- Reverse or waive a specific penalty, fee, or suspension — only the support
  team can decide that (escalate).
- Discuss internal operations, internal staff names, tools, or company data.
